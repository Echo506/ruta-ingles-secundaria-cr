Undécimo · Listening B1

Simulacro de comprensión auditiva

Escuchá los tres audios y respondé las 15 preguntas. Podés reproducir cada audio más de una vez y leer la transcripción si la necesitás.

Tiempo restante 25:00

Instrucciones

  • Tenés 25 minutos para completar el simulacro.
  • Escuchá cada audio con atención antes de responder.
  • Podés usar la transcripción como apoyo, pero intentá primero sin leerla.
  • Seleccioná una respuesta por pregunta.
  • Al finalizar, presioná “Finalizar simulacro” para recibir tu resultado.
  • El contenido es práctica original y no reproduce una prueba oficial.
Listening 1

A Phone Call About a Trip

Escuchá la conversación entre dos amigas planeando un viaje.

Ana: Hey, are you still coming with us this weekend?

Laura: Yes, but I need to know what time the bus leaves.

Ana: It leaves at 7:30 in the morning from the main terminal.

Laura: That's early. Should I bring money for lunch?

Ana: No, lunch is included. Just bring money for snacks and the entrance fee to the park.

Laura: Great, thanks. I'll be there by 7:15.

1. What time does the bus leave?
2. What does Laura need to bring money for?
3. What time will Laura arrive at the terminal?
4. Is lunch included in the trip?
5. What is the general purpose of this conversation?
Listening 2

A School Announcement

Escuchá el anuncio sobre un cambio de horario en la escuela.

Good morning, students. Please remember that tomorrow's science fair will start at 9:00 a.m. instead of 8:00 a.m., because the gym needs extra time for setup. All projects must be ready by 8:30. Students who need help carrying materials should contact Mr. Rojas in the main office before 3:00 p.m. today. Also, remember that parents are welcome to attend, but they must register at the front desk when they arrive. Thank you, and good luck to everyone participating.

6. What time will the science fair start?
7. Why was the schedule changed?
8. Who should students contact for help carrying materials?
9. What must parents do before attending?
10. By what time must projects be ready?
Listening 3

A Job Interview Tip

Escuchá el consejo de una consejera escolar sobre entrevistas de trabajo.

When you go to a job interview, the first thing to remember is to arrive at least ten minutes early. This shows respect for the interviewer's time. Second, bring a printed copy of your résumé, even if you already sent it by email. Third, prepare two or three questions about the company; this shows genuine interest. Finally, avoid checking your phone during the interview, even if you are waiting. Small details like these often matter as much as your answers to the questions.

12. What should you bring even if you already sent your résumé by email?
13. Why should you prepare questions about the company?
14. What should you avoid doing during the interview?
